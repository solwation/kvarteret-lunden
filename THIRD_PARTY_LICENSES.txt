Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (data/elevation.json, src/elevationgrid.js)
================================================================

Contains modified Copernicus data. The ground-height grid is derived from
the Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (30 m, tile N55 E013, AWS Open Data
`copernicus-dem-30m`), (c) DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and (c) Airbus Defence and
Space GmbH 2014-2018, provided under the Copernicus DEM licence with funding
by the European Union under contract COP-DEM-2018. It is resampled to a 10 m
grid (bare-earth estimate) and is used as a height reference only.

Lantmäteriet Markhöjdmodell via kart-api (archived point samples, public #106)
©Lantmäteriet · Markhöjdmodell · CC BY 4.0
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
https://kart-api.se/en/guides/elevation-sweden/
Source responses and attribution preserved in docs/references/elevation-hoje-a/point-api-south-road-2026-10-19.json.
Local relative heights, interpolation and building aprons are modified model estimates;
RH2000 minus14.36m is an assumed model origin, not a surveyed dwelling floor.
